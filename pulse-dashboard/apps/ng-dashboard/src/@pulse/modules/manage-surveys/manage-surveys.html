Understanding Survey Behavior

Pulse Delivery & Rollover

  • Questions are delivered in smaller pulses based on frequency (daily/weekly/fortnightly/monthly)
  • Missed questions roll over to the next pulse automatically
  • Questions are randomly sampled for each employee
  • Surveys close at 23:59:59 on the end date

Reminder System

  • Automatic reminders: Day 3 (first), Day 7 (second)
  • Only sent to employees who have not started the pulse
  • Manual reminders can be triggered anytime
  • No reminders sent if survey is paused or ended

Question Distribution

  • Questions are randomly sampled to prevent survey fatigue
  • Each employee receives a different set of questions per pulse
  • All questions will be distributed by the final pulse
